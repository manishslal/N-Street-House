# What the walkthrough frames (reference/photos/walk_001..058) show

Read from the 58 stills. These are observations, not measurements; nothing here has a tape behind it.

## Condition and finishes of the existing house (now used in `house-existing`)
* Main floor living area and hall: dark grey wood-look plank. Upper floor rooms: bare plywood subfloor. Baths upstairs: lighter grey plank.
* Kitchen: beige vinyl tile, brown wood cabinets, black marble-look counters, black fridge and gas range, white dishwasher, raised pass-through counter to the living room.
* Powder room: brown/beige vinyl tile, pedestal sink, mosaic tile splash. Dining: brass chandelier, subfloor.
* Utility closet at the rear hall: louvered bifold doors, water heater and furnace; stacked washer/dryer behind a panel door (frames 17-21).
* Under-stair closet with the electrical panel (frame 7). Stair: straight, wall-mounted wood handrail, knee wall at the top landing (frames 5, 30).
* Doors are white 2-panel (about 2'6"), brass knobs. Windows have white blinds. Front has a covered side porch with white rail and vinyl siding (frames 56-58).

## Hallways: the model looks too narrow
In the model the main hall runs between interior walls at plan x = 5.27 and 7.1 ft, about 1.8 ft centre to centre. In frames 25-26 the hall is clearly wider than one door leaf (about 2'6"), which suggests roughly 3 to 3.5 ft. The upper hall (frames 41-42) looks similar. This is a visual estimate from a wide-angle phone; I did not move any walls for it.

**Measure these three and I will correct the geometry:** (1) main hall wall to wall at mid height, (2) upper hall wall to wall, (3) distance from the front wall to the first stair riser.
